About DMAP

Purpose and positioning.

DMAP is a centralized platform for managing contracts, contractor personnel, documents, and administrative workflows. It connects government and contractor teams through shared records, coordinated tasks, and clear visibility into ongoing work.

Positioning

A practical tool for complex coordination.

DMAP is a practical tool for simplifying complex coordination, improving oversight, and keeping people and processes organized.

Built by InnoVet, a veteran-founded company in Dover, Delaware that builds teams of military veterans to solve hard problems for the Department of Defense.

Who it supports

Government and contractor teams, working from the same record.

Government program and contract teams

Coordinate contract oversight, personnel activities, and administrative requirements.

Contractor management teams

Maintain employee information, submit required documentation, and track assigned actions.

Security and facilities teams

Participate in applicable personnel, access, and readiness workflows.

Leadership

Review portfolio information, workforce activity, and process status.

Benefits

Less chasing. More finished work.

When contracts, people, and documents share one record, teams spend less time asking where things stand.

  • Centralized information across related activities.
  • Clear ownership of tasks and responsibilities.
  • Better coordination between government and contractor teams.
  • Consistent onboarding, offboarding, and document workflows.
  • Greater visibility into pending work and overall progress.
