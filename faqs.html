What is DMAP?
DMAP is a centralized platform for managing contracts, contractor personnel, documents, and administrative workflows. It connects government and contractor teams through shared records, coordinated tasks, and clear visibility into ongoing work.
What does DMAP support?
Contract and agreement management, contractor employee records, onboarding and offboarding, form submissions, document signatures, and task notifications. See all capabilities
Who uses DMAP?
Government program and contract teams, contractor management teams, security and facilities teams, and leadership. Role-based access gives users the information and actions appropriate to their responsibilities.
How is access controlled?
DMAP uses role-based permissions, authenticated access, and encrypted data protection to support the handling of sensitive operational information.
